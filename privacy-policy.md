## Privacy Policy No Not Again

NoNotAgain does not collect, transmit, or store any personal, browsing, or behavioral data on any server operated by the developer.

- Your notes are saved with Chrome's sync storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). If Chrome Sync is turned on, Chrome syncs them to your Google account so they're available on your other devices. Google handles this under its own privacy policy, and the developer never has access to them.
- Temporary bypass settings are stored locally on your device (`chrome.storage.local`) and are never synced.
- The extension reads the address of the tab you're visiting only to check whether you've set a note for that site. These addresses are never stored or shared.
