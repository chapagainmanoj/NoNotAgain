## Privacy Policy No Not Again

NoNotAgain does not collect, transmit, or store any personal, browsing, or behavioral data on any server operated by the developer. It has no analytics and makes no network requests of its own.

- **Blocked sites and quotes** (the site name, your note, the display setting, and your own quotes) are saved with Chrome's sync storage. If Chrome Sync is turned on, Chrome syncs them to your Google account so they're available on your other devices. Google handles this under its own privacy policy, and the developer never has access to them.
- **Site notes** are stored only on your device (`chrome.storage.local`), together with the name of the site each note belongs to (for example `github.com`). They are never synced or sent anywhere. Export files are created only when you ask for one and are saved wherever you choose.
- **Temporary bypass settings** are stored locally on your device and are never synced.
- The extension reads the address of the tab you're visiting only to check whether you've blocked that site or saved notes for it, and to show the note count. Page addresses are never stored or shared.
